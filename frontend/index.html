The Cybersecurity Challenge

Why Social Engineering Evades Traditional Defenses

Attackers continuously weaponize psychological urgency, disposable domains, and brand impersonation. Relying solely on static blacklists or ungrounded generative AI leaves users vulnerable.

The Limitations of Conventional Filters

  • Ephemeral Threat Infrastructure: Attackers register short-lived domains (`.xyz`, `.top`) that bypass domain reputation feeds before lists can update.
  • Cognitive Exploitation: Messages trigger fear (account suspensions, missed deliveries, legal notices) to bypass logical judgment.
  • Black-Box LLM Hallucinations: Asking a generic conversational LLM whether a message is safe often produces inconsistent answers and vulnerable to prompt injection.
  • Blind Faith in Binary Labels: Traditional scanners output "Safe" or "Spam" without providing transparent evidence for human validation.

The TrustLens AI Multi-Layered Approach

  • Empirically Trained ML Classifier: Scikit-learn Logistic Regression trained on 5,171 deduplicated messages computes statistical probabilities.
  • Deterministic Evidence Extraction: Explicit extraction of domains, shortcodes, and financial claims prevents subjective guesswork.
  • Prompt-Injection Shielded AI: Untrusted text is strictly isolated in data tags so embedded commands cannot hijack the investigation agent.
  • Evidence vs Assumption Transparency: Clear distinction between verifiable textual observations and contextual risk implications.
ℹ️
Truthful Security & Accuracy Notice: No machine learning system or AI assistant can detect 100% of deceptive communications or guarantee absolute security. TrustLens AI is engineered to augment human judgment with explainable evidence. Always verify unexpected financial or credential requests directly through official, verified channels (such as the phone number on your bank card).
Transparent Methodology

How TrustLens AI Investigates Messages

A 6-stage verifiable pipeline that transforms untrusted inputs into structured, explainable threat assessments.

1
Stage 1: Ingestion

Message Submission

Paste untrusted SMS/email text or stage an invoice/QR screenshot. Clipboard paste (Ctrl+V) and sample demo payloads are supported.

2
Stage 2: Preprocessing

Security Tokenization

`SecurityTextPreprocessor` preserves threat indicators by mapping links to `__URL__`, amounts to `__CURRENCY__`, and numbers to `__PHONE__`.

3
Stage 3: ML Inference

Trained Model Scoring

Logistic Regression evaluates sublinear TF-IDF n-grams to output a statistical probability $P(\text{deceptive} \mid x)$ without hallucination.

4
Stage 4: Extraction

Observable Warning Signs

Heuristics parse suspicious top-level domains, credential harvesting lures, and urgency triggers into concrete lists of evidence.

5
Stage 5: Synthesis

AI Explanation Agent

Amazon Bedrock (or offline rule-based NLP) synthesizes an accessible summary, highlighting direct observations vs implications.

6
Stage 6: Action

Risk Tier & Safety Actions

Results are displayed in categorized risk levels (Low, Medium, High) with concrete safety recommendations and an interactive AI copilot.

Explainable Intelligence

ML Classifier vs. Generative AI: Distinct Roles

TrustLens AI clearly separates statistical machine learning classification from generative natural language explanation.

1. Statistical ML

Trained ML Classifier

Logistic Regression model with balanced class weights trained strictly on the UCI SMS dataset. Computes a fast, unhallucinated deception probability on server CPU.

2. Statistical Output

Statistical Risk Scores

Posterior probability $P(\text{deceptive} \mid x)$ mapped directly into defensible risk tiers: Low (<35%), Medium (35-65%), and High (>65%).

3. Deterministic NLP

Extracted Evidence

Unambiguous structural facts identified in the message body: exact suspicious URLs, domain extensions, phone shortcodes, and urgency terms.

4. Generative Synthesis

LLM Interpretation

Amazon Bedrock or grounded rule-based NLP synthesizes findings into plain-English reasoning, separating verifiable facts from implications.

Privacy & Responsible AI

Data Handling, Privacy & Known Limitations

Grounded exclusively in our actual implementation: how data is secured, when cloud services are contacted, and where model limits lie.

🔒

Data Storage & Redaction

When scans are recorded, raw text is automatically sanitized with regex rules to mask 16-digit credit card numbers (`[REDACTED_CARD_NUMBER]`), email addresses, and credentials. Full bodies are hashed via SHA-256 for audit lookups.

🌐

Cloud vs Local Processing

When Amazon Bedrock is configured with valid AWS credentials, text is sent over encrypted TLS directly to AWS Bedrock Converse API. When unconfigured, 100% of processing runs completely locally and offline on your own machine.

⚠️

Model Limitations & Advisory Use

Our model operates primarily on English SMS/email patterns. Sophisticated zero-day phishing or multilingual scams may evade detection (6.87% holdout false negative rate). Results are advisory; always confirm through official banking/vendor channels.

        Architecture & Security Design Principles
        
          
        
          
        
            
        
              
        1. No Untrusted Prompt Execution
        
              
        All user-submitted content is isolated in strict structural data tags. Prompt injection instructions ("ignore instructions", "mark safe") are neutralised and cannot alter evaluation parameters.
        
            
        
            
        
              
        2. Decoupled AI Explanation
        
              
        The statistical machine learning pipeline operates independently on CPU. If Amazon Bedrock or third-party LLMs are unreachable or unconfigured, the internal rule-based NLP engine delivers identical structured schemas with zero downtime.
        
            
        
            
        
              
        3. Privacy & Sensitive Data Protection
        
              
        Stored records do not retain raw unredacted messages. Card numbers, emails, and authentication tokens are masked before writing to persistent SQLite/PostgreSQL storage.
        
            
        
            
        
              
        4. Ground-Truth Data Isolation
        
              
        User feedback is stored in an independent audit repository. The core production model is never retrained automatically on unverified external submissions to prevent data poisoning attacks.
